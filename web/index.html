gita.

— every agent needs a direction.

A little experiment: git, but the things it tells an agent are structural instead of textual. Same content-addressed store underneath — just trades unified-diff hunks for ops on named symbols. Three commands, that's the whole show.

# scenario:

01 · diff

show me what changed, not which bytes moved.

Same edit, two readings. The left is what an agent stares at today; the right is what it actually wants.

git diff — users.py
gita diff — users.py

02 · explain

commits remember what they meant.

Each commit stores its manifest as a content-addressed blob next to the tree. gita explain just reads it back — no re-deriving, no re-parsing, no LLM guessing from a diff.

gita

03 · ask

walk the symbol, not the lines.

History is queryable by thing. Who renamed this function? Who calls it now? Answers come from stored manifests plus one CST pass.
